title=Build Ahead
description=[h1]Build Ahead[/h1]
description=Plan your construction ahead. Every click with the build cursor becomes a [b]planned site[/b], drawn as a blue ghost, and your character builds the planned sites one after another, nearest first. Keep clicking while you are still hammering: the clicks queue up instead of being rejected.
description=
description=[h2]Features[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Plan while building[/b]: click as many spots as you like during a build. Each becomes a ghost and is built next, nearest first, within 20 tiles.
description=[*][b]Plan without materials[/b]: sites can be planned before you have the materials. A note tells you what is missing.
description=[*][b]Fetches materials[/b]: what a site still lacks is picked up from the floor, crates, corpses and car trunks within 20 tiles. Locked containers, other players' safehouses and places you cannot reach are left alone. It carries only what is needed, stays under the weight that would hurt you, fills worn bags first, and builds straight from the inventory when everything fits in one trip.
description=[*][b]Material bans[/b]: click the small X on a material in the build window to never use that item type (e.g. keep your planks, use logs). Per player.
description=[*][b]Never walls you in[/b]: the chain builds a closing wall from the outside, or leaves it planned. If you want to lock yourself in, right-click the ghost and choose "Build planned".
description=[*][b]Upgrades ahead[/b]: plan a wall on a planned wall frame, a door in a planned door frame. They are built in order.
description=[*][b]Ghost colors[/b]: blue = can be built, yellow = materials or skill missing, red = blocked, orange = would wall you in.
description=[*][b]Right-click a ghost[/b]: "Build planned" starts from that site (greyed out with the reason when it cannot be built), "Cancel" removes it. Anyone can cancel any site.
description=[*]Building starts only when you click, when a build finishes, or from "Build planned". Nothing starts on its own while you are idle. Moving stops the chain; the sites stay.
description=[*]Sites are saved with the world.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]Multiplayer[/h2]
description=The server keeps the planned sites and shares them with everyone. Any player who finishes a build nearby continues with the nearest site; a site someone is building is left alone by others. Sites you lack the skill for are skipped. The mod must be installed on the server and on every client.
description=
description=[h2]Compatibility[/h2]
description=Build 42. Works with the build window's buildings (B42 build entities). No vanilla files replaced. Safe to add to existing saves. Mods that also change build placement or action queues may conflict.
description=
description=[h2]한국어[/h2]
description=건설 커서로 클릭한 자리가 모두 [b]건설 예정지[/b](파란 고스트)가 되고, 캐릭터가 가까운 순서로 차례대로 지어요. 짓는 중에도 계속 클릭해서 예약할 수 있고, 재료가 없어도 예정지를 찍어둘 수 있어요. 부족한 재료는 20칸 안의 바닥, 상자, 시체, 차량 트렁크에서 가져와요(잠긴 곳, 남의 안전구역, 갈 수 없는 곳 제외). 부상 없는 무게까지만 들고, 착용한 가방부터 채워요. 건설 창 재료 칸의 X 버튼으로 특정 재료를 쓰지 않게 할 수 있어요. 자동으로 지을 때는 캐릭터가 벽 안에 갇히지 않게 바깥에서 지어요(일부러 갇히려면 고스트 우클릭 "예정지 건설"). 고스트 색: 파랑 = 지을 수 있음, 노랑 = 재료/스킬 부족, 빨강 = 막힘, 주황 = 갇힐 수 있음. 멀티에서는 서버가 예정지를 공유하며, 서버와 모든 클라이언트에 모드가 필요해요.

In vanilla you can only place the next construction once the current one is finished and you hold all the materials. Build Ahead lets you plan ahead: every click with the build cursor becomes a [b]planned site[/b], drawn as a ghost, and your character builds the planned sites one after another, nearest first.

[b]Keep clicking while you are still building, or before you have the materials: the clicks are kept as planned sites instead of being rejected.[/b]

[h2]Features[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Plan while building:[/b] click as many spots as you like during a build. When a build finishes, the nearest buildable site within 20 tiles is built next.
[*][b]Plan without materials:[/b] sites can be planned before you have the materials. A note over your character names what is missing.
[*][b]Fetches materials:[/b] what a site still lacks is picked up within 20 tiles of it, from the floor, containers, corpses and vehicle trunks. Locked containers, other players' safehouses and places you cannot reach are left alone.
[*][b]Fewer trips:[/b] materials go into your main inventory, never into worn bags (moving items in and out of a bag is slow), up to the most the inventory holds, so you may be overloaded on the way. A trip with room to spare also brings the materials for the next sites in the order they will be built, so a row of floors or crates takes a few trips instead of one per site. Materials already lying next to a site are used where they lie.
[*][b]Tools:[/b] a tool taken from a container goes back where it came from when the chain ends, and the weapon you held is equipped again.
[*][b]Material bans:[/b] click the small X on a material in the build window to never use that item type for planned builds (e.g. keep your planks, use logs). Click it again to allow it. Bans are per player.
[*][b]Never walls you in:[/b] a wall that would close you in is built from the outside, or left planned. To build it anyway, right-click its ghost and choose [b]Build planned[/b].
[*][b]Upgrades ahead:[/b] plan a wall or window frame on a planned wall frame, or a door in a planned door frame. They are built in order.
[*][b]Stacked crates:[/b] click the same square again to plan the next wooden crate on top, as vanilla stacks them (up to three). They are built from the bottom up.
[/list]

[h2]Ghosts[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Blue:[/b] can be built.
[*][b]Yellow:[/b] materials missing, or waiting for the crate below.
[*][b]Red:[/b] blocked.
[*][b]Orange:[/b] building it would wall you in.
[/list]
Right-click a ghost: [b]Build planned[/b] starts the chain from that site (greyed out with the reason when it cannot be built), [b]Cancel planned build[/b] removes it, and [b]Cancel all planned builds nearby[/b] clears the sites around you. Anyone can cancel any site.

Building starts only when you click, when a build finishes, or from [b]Build planned[/b]. Nothing starts on its own while you are idle. Moving or starting another action stops the chain; the sites stay planned. Sites are saved with the world.

[h2]Multiplayer[/h2]
The server keeps the planned sites and shares them with everyone. Any player who finishes a build nearby continues with the nearest site, and a site someone is already building is left to them. Sites you lack the skill for are skipped. The mod must be installed on the server and on every client.

[h2]Compatibility[/h2]
[list]
[*]Works with the buildings in the Build 42 build window. No vanilla files are replaced.
[*]Safe to add to an existing save. Removing it drops the planned sites.
[*]Mods that also change build placement or the action queue may conflict.
[/list]
